*Register an unprecedented number of submissions made from multiple sources on curtailing alcohol sales in the inner city.* 

Title: Research Analyst  
Department: Strategy & Planning  
Organisation: Christchurch City Council  

Technology:  

- NVivo  
- MS Access forms and reports, MS SQL Server database engine.

Achievements:  

- Registered over 4000 public submissions and reported to council.  

Outcomes:  
  
- Policy Team, Strategic Policy Unit, Monitoring and Research Team, Transport and Research Unit, [‘Draft Local Alcohol Policy 2013: Summary and analysis of submissions received during the Special Consultative Procedure, from 31 May - 1 July 2013’](https://resources.ccc.govt.nz/files/TheCouncil/policiesreportsstrategies/policies/groups/alcohol/DraftLAPSummaryandanalysisofsubmissionsJuly2013.pdf). Prepared by the Christchurch City Council, July 2013. 

---

The Local Alcohol Policy consultation required several Facebook surveys by the Liquor Council to be included along with the official submissions. The output was a word-searchable pdf report the policy analysts could use for manual coding. 
 
- Built MS Access forms & reports, SQL Server backend database. 
- 4,060 submissions received on the Draft Local Alcohol Policy from multiple sources: 1,053 submissions were received through the Council website, 1,929 submissions were received through the Hospitality New Zealand survey on Facebook, and 1,0708 submissions were received from a Chch Late petition on Facebook. 
- Charts and Graphs were printed on pages 8-49 of the report to Council.
