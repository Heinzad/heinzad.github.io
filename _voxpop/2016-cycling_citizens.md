*Record your route in Open Street Map with OSM Tracker*  

Help other cyclists and have a go at contributing to OpenStreetMap. The information you provide will be incorporated into maps and can be used in giving directions for people who need to know how to cycle safely from A to B.

<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/osm-screenshot.jpg" alt="OpenStreetMap" class="full">


OSMTracker makes it easy to record your route when editing cycle ways in Open Street Map.

I had a go in using it to trace my ride along the [“Papanui Parallel / Puari ki Papanui"](https://www.openstreetmap.org/user/Adam%20Heinz/diary) cycleway in Christchurch. I didn't even need to read the instructions.

I had a go. Why don't you?
